KaTeX 0.19.1
Source: https://katex.org/
Distribution: https://registry.npmjs.org/katex/-/katex-0.19.1.tgz
License: MIT (full license in public/vendor/katex/LICENSE)

Bundled browser assets and fonts support offline equation rendering.
The JavaScript and WOFF2 fonts are unmodified. The CSS font source lists
have been reduced to WOFF2 only, matching the bundled font files.
The npm archive is SHA-512 verified before extracting an explicit asset
allowlist; its integrity and each bundled asset hash are recorded in
public/vendor/katex/vendor-manifest.json.

To reproduce: node scripts/vendor-katex.mjs
